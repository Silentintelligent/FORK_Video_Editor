# FORK Privacy Policy

Last updated: <DATE>   ·   Contact: <YOUR-EMAIL>

FORK is a video editor that works on your device.

**What we collect:** nothing. FORK has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising and no servers of our own. Your videos, photos, audio, projects
and exports stay on your device and are never uploaded by FORK.

**Internet use:** the app asks for internet permission only so you can
download optional AI model files (for captions, voices and background removal)
from Settings. These files are fetched from public hosts (huggingface.co,
github.com). Those services can see your IP address as for any download,
under their own privacy policies. FORK sends them no personal data.

**Files you choose:** you pick media through Android's system file picker.
FORK reads only the files you select and writes exports to the folder shown
in the app (Movies/FORK).

**Voice features:** speech recognition, text-to-speech and voice cloning run
on the device. Recordings and voice samples are not sent anywhere. Do not
clone or imitate anyone's voice without their consent.

**Children:** FORK is not directed to children under 13.
